Payment, Cancellation and Refund Policy

Effective date: {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}}

This Policy explains how payments for the dooCall service are made, how you can stop using the service, and when and how money is refunded. The service is provided by {{COMPANY_NAME}} (the "Provider"). The Policy is part of the Terms and Conditions and should be read together with the Privacy Policy.

1. What you pay for

1.1. dooCall is a subscription service for companies: recording, storing and analysing the calls of your operators. The service is entirely digital; nothing is shipped physically.

1.2. The price is set per active operator per month according to the tariff published on the website. All prices and settlements are in Uzbek soums (UZS).

1.3. Every new company receives a free trial of 14 days. Nothing is charged during the trial and no bank card is required to start it.

2. How payment works

2.1. Payment is made by topping up a prepaid balance in your account. You choose the amount yourself.

2.2. You can top up the balance:

  • online by bank card through the payment provider Paylov. The payment is confirmed by your bank with a one-time SMS code (3-D Secure). The Provider does not store your full card number (for a linked card see section 8);
  • by bank transfer or in cash against an invoice. Such payments are credited after they are confirmed, normally within 1 working day.

2.3. A payment is credited to the balance only after the payment provider confirms it. A failed or declined payment is not charged. If your bank shows a temporary hold on the amount, the bank releases it according to its own timing.

2.4. The fee is deducted from the balance every day for each active operator. You control your spending by activating or deactivating operators in your account; the change takes effect the same day.

2.5. Access to the service is provided immediately after the balance is credited.

2.6. Bank commissions for transferring money are borne by the payer, unless the law provides otherwise.

3. Cancellation

3.1. You may stop using the service at any time: deactivate your operators or simply do not top up the balance. There is no cancellation fee and no minimum term.

3.2. Access continues for as long as the balance covers the daily fee. When the balance runs out, access is suspended until the next top-up; we warn you in advance.

3.3. Either party may terminate the agreement by notifying the other party 10 working days in advance.

4. When refunds are not made

4.1. Amounts already deducted for days of service that have been used are not refundable.

4.2. If you stop using the service voluntarily, the unused prepaid balance is not paid out in money. It stays on your account and can be used to pay for the service.

5. When we refund in full

5.1. We refund the relevant amount in full in the following cases:

  • a duplicate or erroneous payment;
  • money was charged but not credited to your balance;
  • the amount charged is greater than the amount you confirmed;
  • the service was unavailable through the Provider's fault for more than 3 consecutive days. In this case the compensation is calculated in proportion to the downtime and, at your choice, is either credited to the balance or refunded.

6. How to request a refund

6.1. Send a request to {{EMAIL}} from the email address registered in your account. Please state:

  • the name of your company;
  • the date and amount of the payment;
  • the last 4 digits of the card or the transaction ID;
  • the reason for the refund.

6.2. We review the request within 5 working days and reply by email.

6.3. An approved refund is sent to the same card or account that was used for the payment, within 10 banking days. The actual crediting time depends on the issuing bank and is typically up to 30 days.

6.4. Card payments are not refunded in cash.

7. Disputed payments (chargebacks)

7.1. If you do not recognise a payment or disagree with it, please contact our support first — in most cases we resolve the matter faster than a bank dispute procedure.

7.2. If a dispute is opened with the bank, the account may be suspended until the dispute is resolved.

8. Automatic payments

8.1. You may link a UzCard or Humo card in your account. The card is confirmed once with an SMS code from your bank. Card data is processed and stored by the payment provider Paylov; the Provider keeps only a card token, the masked number (its last digits), the cardholder name as returned by the payment provider, and the expiry date.

8.2. Auto-payment is optional and switched off by default. Only the administrator of your company can switch it on in the account, by ticking an explicit consent box. This consent is your standing instruction and authorisation to charge the linked card without an additional SMS code.

8.3. A charge is made when the balance covers less than one day of service at the current number of active operators — no more than one attempt per day. You choose the amount: the cost of one month of service at the current number of active operators (the default) or a fixed amount. Charges never exceed the monthly limit shown in your account — by default twice the monthly cost; you may set a different limit.

8.4. If a charge fails (for example, because of insufficient funds), we retry on the following days, for up to 3 consecutive days; after that auto-payment is switched off and you are notified in your account. If the card is blocked, has expired or has been removed, auto-payment is switched off immediately. The suspension rules for an empty balance (clause 3.2) continue to apply.

8.5. Every automatic charge is shown in your account — as a notification and in the payment history. You can switch auto-payment off or remove the card at any time; this takes effect immediately for future charges.

8.6. Refunds of automatic charges follow this Policy. An automatic charge that you consider erroneous — for example, made after auto-payment was switched off or above the limit — is refunded in full on request under the procedure in section 6.

9. Governing law

9.1. This Policy is governed by the legislation of the Republic of Uzbekistan, including the Civil Code and the Law "On protection of consumer rights" where it applies. Nothing in this Policy limits rights that the law grants you and that cannot be waived.
