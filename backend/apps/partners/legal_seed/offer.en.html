Public Offer for Partners of the dooCall Service

Version effective from {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}}

This document is an official public offer of {{COMPANY_NAME}} (the "Company"), the operator of the dooCall service, addressed to individuals and legal entities who wish to cooperate with the Company as an integrator or a sales manager (the "Partner"). Accepting this offer in the manner described in clause 1.3 creates a binding agreement between the Company and the Partner (the "Agreement").

1. General provisions

1.1. Service means the dooCall cloud service for recording, storing and analysing telephone calls, including the websites doocall.uz and app.doocall.uz, the mobile application and the personal cabinets.

1.2. Customer means a company registered in the Service that pays the Company for its use. Partner cabinet means the Partner's personal section of the Service, where attached Customers, accruals, the balance and payout requests are displayed.

1.3. The offer is accepted by pressing the "Accept" button in the profile section of the Partner cabinet. The date of acceptance and the accepted version are recorded by the Service. From that moment the Partner is deemed to have read, understood and unconditionally accepted all terms of this offer.

1.4. The Agreement does not create an employment relationship, a joint venture or a representative office. The Partner acts independently, in its own name and at its own expense, and is not authorised to conclude contracts, accept payments or assume obligations on behalf of the Company.

2. Subject of the Agreement

2.1. The Partner undertakes to attract Customers to the Service and, where applicable, to assist them with connection and set-up, and the Company undertakes to pay the Partner remuneration for the Customers' successful payments on the terms of this Agreement.

2.2. Contracts for the use of the Service are concluded directly between the Company and the Customer. All Customer payments are made to the Company only.

3. Partner roles and attachment of Customers

3.1. Integrator. A Customer is attached to an Integrator if it registered using the Integrator's personal referral code or link, or was registered by the Integrator from the Partner cabinet. A Customer may be attached to one Integrator only.

3.2. Sales manager. A Sales manager supervises the Integrators assigned to them by the Company and receives remuneration from the payments of the Customers attached to those Integrators.

3.3. The attachment of a Customer to an Integrator, and of an Integrator to a Sales manager, is recorded in the Service. The Company may change an attachment where it was made by mistake, in breach of this Agreement, or at the Customer's reasoned request. Remuneration accrued before the change remains with the Partner to whom it was accrued.

4. Remuneration: calculation and accrual

4.1. The Integrator's remuneration (cashback) is calculated as a percentage of each successful payment made by an attached Customer. The Sales manager's remuneration (commission) is calculated as a percentage of each successful payment made by Customers of the Integrators assigned to them.

4.2. Remuneration is accrued only for payments made within a limited period counted from the date of the Customer's registration in the Service. Payments made after that period do not give rise to remuneration.

4.3. The percentage rate, the length of the period under clause 4.2 and the minimum payout amount are set in the platform settings and are displayed in the Partner cabinet. At the date of this version the standard values are: Integrator cashback — 40%, period — 12 months, minimum payout — 50,000 UZS. The Sales manager's rate is shown in their cabinet. The Company may set an individual rate for a particular Partner.

4.4. Remuneration is accrued automatically at the moment the Customer's payment is confirmed in the Service, at the rate in force for the Partner at that moment, and is shown in the Partner cabinet. Remuneration is accrued only while the Partner's account is active.

4.5. If a Customer's payment is refunded or cancelled, the remuneration accrued for it is reversed. If the reversed amount has already been paid out, the Company may offset it against the Partner's subsequent accruals or require its return.

4.6. No remuneration is due for a Customer's trial period, for unpaid invoices, or for payments obtained in breach of clause 6.2.

5. Payouts

5.1. The Partner's available balance is the sum of accrued and not yet paid remuneration less the amounts of payout requests under review.

5.2. A payout is made at the Partner's request submitted from the Partner cabinet. The requested amount may not be less than the minimum payout amount or more than the available balance.

5.3. The request is reviewed by the Company's administration and, if approved, is paid to the bank card or account details entered by the Partner in the profile, normally within 10 (ten) working days. The status of the request is shown in the Partner cabinet. If a request is rejected, the amount returns to the available balance and the reason is communicated to the Partner.

5.4. The Partner is responsible for the accuracy and currency of its payment details. The Company is not liable for delay or non-receipt of funds caused by incorrect details.

5.5. The Partner independently calculates and pays all taxes and other mandatory payments on the remuneration received. Where the law obliges the Company to act as a tax agent, the Company withholds and reports the relevant amounts, and the payout is reduced accordingly.

6. Rights and obligations of the Partner

6.1. The Partner has the right to use the referral code and link, to register Customers from the Partner cabinet, to see the commercial data of its Customers and its accruals, and to request payouts under section 5.

6.2. The Partner undertakes:

  • to present the Service, its functions and prices truthfully, in accordance with the information published by the Company;
  • not to send spam, not to use misleading or unlawful advertising, and not to use the Company's name, logo or trademarks in a way that suggests the Partner is the Company or its official representative;
  • not to attach itself or its affiliated persons as Customers for the purpose of obtaining remuneration, and not to register fictitious Customers;
  • not to make promises, give guarantees or grant discounts on behalf of the Company;
  • to keep its login credentials secure and to bear responsibility for all actions performed under its account;
  • to comply with the legislation of the Republic of Uzbekistan, including the rules on advertising and personal data.

6.3. The Partner may upload its name and logo to the profile. If the Partner enables public display, the Company may show them in the "Our integrators" section of the website free of charge.

7. Rights and obligations of the Company

7.1. The Company undertakes to provide access to the Partner cabinet, to record attachments and accruals correctly, and to make payouts under section 5.

7.2. The Company may change the percentage rates, the period under clause 4.2 and the minimum payout amount. Changes apply to future accruals only and are communicated through the Partner cabinet.

7.3. The Company may suspend the Partner's account, withhold or cancel remuneration and refuse a payout if the remuneration was obtained by fraud or in breach of this Agreement, until the circumstances are clarified.

7.4. The Company independently determines the prices and functions of the Service and the terms for Customers, and may refuse to serve any Customer or terminate its service in accordance with the Customer agreement.

8. Data and confidentiality

8.1. The Partner has access only to the commercial data of its Customers: name, status, number of operators and accruals. The Partner has no access to call recordings, contacts or other content of Customers.

8.2. The parties keep confidential any non-public information received in connection with the Agreement and do not disclose it to third parties, except where disclosure is required by law.

8.3. By accepting the offer, the Partner consents to the processing of the personal data provided in the profile for the purposes of performing the Agreement and making payouts.

9. Liability

9.1. The parties are liable for breach of the Agreement in accordance with the legislation of the Republic of Uzbekistan.

9.2. The Company's total liability to the Partner is limited to the amount of remuneration accrued and not paid to the Partner. The Company is not liable for lost profit or for interruptions of the Service.

9.3. The Partner compensates the Company for losses caused by the Partner's breach of clause 6.2, including claims of Customers and third parties.

10. Term, amendment and termination

10.1. The Agreement takes effect at the moment of acceptance and is concluded for an indefinite term.

10.2. The Company may amend this offer by publishing a new version in the Service. To continue cooperation, the Partner must accept the new version in the profile.

10.3. Either party may terminate the Agreement at any time by notifying the other party through the Partner cabinet or by e-mail. Upon termination, the accrued and undisputed balance that is not less than the minimum payout amount is paid to the Partner under section 5; no new remuneration is accrued after termination.

11. Governing law and disputes

11.1. The Agreement is governed by the legislation of the Republic of Uzbekistan, including the provisions of the Civil Code on public offers and on services rendered in the interests of another party.

11.2. Disputes are resolved by negotiation. If no agreement is reached within 30 (thirty) calendar days from the receipt of a written claim, the dispute is referred to the competent court at the Company's location.
